# Chapter Revision Plan

**Basis:** D-03 (`PhD DETAILED CONCEPT NOTE 2026_CHECK`, 199 pp., 68,011 words), D-04 (proposal structure), D-05/D-06 (briefs), D-01/D-02 (supervisory papers).
**Rule:** original meaning and research direction are preserved. Where a change alters meaning, it is flagged as a **supervisor decision**, not made silently.

---

## 1. The structural decision that must precede all editing

The draft has two numbering systems and no theoretical framework chapter. Inserting Chapter 3 forces a choice.

### Option A — Theory-led architecture (recommended)

| Ch | Title | Source in draft |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Introduction and background | Block B (pp. 1–20), revised |
| 2 | Literature review | Block C (pp. 21–116), revised and re-scoped |
| **3** | **Theoretical framework (Posthumanism)** | **New — to be written** |
| 4 | Conceptual framework and the SETILL model | Block D (pp. 117–140), rewritten to align with Ch3 |
| 5 | Research design and methodology | Block E (pp. 141–186), revised |
| 6 | Results / findings | Missing — to be written from data |
| 7 | Discussion, contribution and conclusion | Partly in Block F |

**Advantages:** matches the client's brief exactly (Chapter 3 = theory); matches the proposal guideline's order (theory §5, conceptual §6, methodology §7); gives posthumanism the architectural prominence the supervisor intends; separates theory from conceptual model, which the brief explicitly demands.

**Cost:** every cross-reference in 68,000 words must be renumbered. This is unavoidable and is best done once, at the end, with automated numbering.

### Option B — Keep conceptual framework as Chapter 3, add theory as Chapter 2b

Rejected. It subordinates the supervisor's theory to the model and reproduces the very confusion the brief asks to avoid ("Do not confuse it with the theoretical framework").

### Option C — Merge theory and conceptual framework into one chapter

Rejected on the same grounds, and because it would make Chapter 3 unmanageably long (theory + model + SETILL).

**Recommendation: Option A.** All subsequent planning assumes Option A.

### Chapter 3 — Theoretical framework (new)

Full structure and argument map: `04_theoretical_framework/chapter3_structure_and_argument_map.md`.
Headline decisions:

- Posthumanism is the **primary** foundation (supervisor's instruction), developed through Braidotti (nomadic subject, posthuman knowledge, affirmative ethics), Haraway (cyborg, situated knowledges, sympoiesis, response-ability), Barad (agential realism, intra-action, apparatus, diffraction) and Hayles (informational/posthuman embodiment, the cognitive nonconscious).
- Supporting perspectives are **adjudicated, not accumulated**: systems thinking (compatible, supplies structure), socio-technical theory (compatible, supplies levels and transition dynamics), energy justice (tension — anthropocentric — but necessary for normative operationalisation), Living Lab theory (compatible, supplies method), TAM (partially incompatible — treat as contrast).
