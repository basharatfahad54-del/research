## 5. What is complete

1. **Chapter 3** now addresses all four school requirements explicitly: the general
   importance of a theoretical framework (not topic-specific); the main theories;
   how they relate to the research problem; and a justification keyed to the
   research questions and expected findings. It contains a summary assessment
   table and a five-column RQ-alignment matrix, and closes with five propositions
   that Chapter 4 operationalises.
2. **Chapter 4** converts the theory into a researchable model: ten constructs,
   eight proposed relationships, ten SETILL components, and two editable vector
   figures.
3. **Abstract** drafted to the school's 250–300-word requirement and its four
   required elements.
4. **Reference files** generated from verified metadata only, in three formats.
5. **Compliance, verification and QA reports** produced so the remaining gaps are
   visible rather than buried.

## 6. What is not complete

1. **AI-use permission (D10).** No prose can be declared submission-ready.
2. **Citation style (D9).** In-text citation placeholders (`{Author, Year #ID}`)
   remain; EndNote record IDs are placeholders, not real library IDs.
3. **Chapter 3 length (D11).** 5,835 body words against a 3,500–4,500 working budget.
   Trimming options are listed in `07_QA_AND_OUTSTANDING_ISSUES.md`.
4. **Reference coverage.** 40 of 49 matrix rows still record "TBC" as identifier.
   The full bibliography cannot be finalised until these are checked at source.
5. **Chapters 1 and 5 alignment.** Chapter 3 and Chapter 4 are consistent with each
   other. A static scan of Chapters 1, 2 and 5 found none of the old concept note's
   contamination (no Umuahia/Nigeria material, no past-tense fieldwork claims, no
   overclaiming), so the remaining risk is conceptual alignment, not contamination.
   A line-by-line human read is still required (see `05_TERMINOLOGY_CHANGE_LOG.md`).
6. **Figure and table numbering.** Numbering differs between the long-form draft
   parts and the new chapter drafts; it must be regenerated at assembly.
7. **DOCX/PDF build (K).** Blocked on missing Python packages and stale hard-coded
   paths.
