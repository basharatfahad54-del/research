## 1. Verification protocol (to be applied to every source)

| Step | Action | Evidence required |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Extract the in-text citation exactly as it appears | Raw string + page number |
| 2 | Search Crossref by DOI, then by bibliographic query | DOI resolved or documented as not found |
| 3 | Confirm author order, year, title, journal, volume, issue, pages | Match all fields |
| 4 | Record the DOI | Must resolve at `https://doi.org/…` |
| 5 | Confirm the source is peer-reviewed / accredited (D-04 §12) | Journal indexing or publisher record |
| 6 | Obtain full text lawfully (open access, repository, author manuscript, institutional subscription) | File in `/research_work/references/` or a logged "not accessible" note |
| 7 | Read the relevant section before quoting or closely paraphrasing | Page numbers recorded |
| 8 | Enter into EndNote; export to `research_references.ris` | RIS record |

**Rules of record:**
- A "found" result in Crossref is **not** proof that the citation in the draft is correct. The draft's own author/year must be checked against the record; discrepancies are logged.
- Where a source cannot be located, it is recorded in `missing_references.md` with the exact in-text string and the search attempts made. It is **not** reconstructed by guesswork.
- Quotations already present in the draft must be checked against the original page before they are retained. Unverifiable quotations are removed.

This is the immediate intellectual lineage of the present PhD. Two consequences follow:

1. **The literature review can be rebuilt around a real, identifiable body of work** rather than scattered citations. That is a major strength, currently invisible because the draft cites these authors without a bibliography.
2. **The novelty claim must be sharpened.** The GENS programme has already produced: a gender analysis framework (Anditi et al. 2022), living labs for gendered energy innovation (Mukama et al. 2022), a co-design toolkit (Petrulaityte et al. 2022), and systems modelling (Batinge et al. 2022). The present study cannot claim to be "first" in any of those areas. Its defensible novelty is the **posthumanist theoretical foundation + just-transition framing + energy acceptance as organising construct + South African informal settlement focus**. This must be stated precisely in Chapter 1 and Chapter 3 §3.11.
